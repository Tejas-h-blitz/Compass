Mediterranean Diet Nutrition Science and Cardiovascular Wellness
=================================================================

Nutritional Fundamentals:
The traditional Mediterranean diet is globally recognized as one of the healthiest dietary patterns. It emphasizes whole foods, extra virgin olive oil as the primary fat source, fresh vegetables, legumes, and moderate wild-caught seafood consumption.

Key Cardiovascular Health Benefits:
- Omega-3 Fatty Acids: Found in wild salmon, mackerel, and walnuts, reducing systemic inflammation.
- Polyphenol Antioxidants: Abundant in cold-pressed olive oil, improving vascular endothelial function.
- Low Glycemic Index: Focus on lentils, chickpeas, and ancient grains stabilizes blood sugar levels.

Sample Weekly Dinner Plan:
- Monday: Grilled Atlantic salmon with asparagus, quinoa, and lemon vinaigrette.
- Tuesday: Greek salad with organic feta, Kalamata olives, cucumbers, and tomatoes.
- Wednesday: Tuscan white bean stew with rosemary, garlic, and sourdough.
