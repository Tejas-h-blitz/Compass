Weekly Student Meal Prep Routine:

Batch 1: Overnight Oats (Sunday Evening Prep)
Combine rolled oats, chia seeds, oat milk, peanut butter, and a dash of cinnamon into 4 glass mason jars. Refrigerate overnight; top with sliced bananas or blueberries before morning classes.

Batch 2: High-Protein Sheet Pan Fajita Bowls
Dice chicken breast (or extra-firm tofu), bell peppers, and red onions into bite-sized strips. Toss in olive oil, smoked paprika, cumin, garlic powder, and lime juice. Roast on baking sheet at 400 degrees Fahrenheit for 25 minutes. Serve over brown rice with black beans and salsa.

Quick Exam Night Ramen Upgrade:
Boil standard instant noodles with vegetable broth. Whisk in a spoonful of miso paste, sesame oil, and sriracha. Drop in a soft-boiled egg and fresh green onions.
