Campus Recreation Center Fitness Routine:

Monday - Upper Body Push:
- Standard push-ups: 4 sets of 20 reps
- Diamond push-ups: 3 sets of 15 reps
- Parallel bar dips: 4 sets of 12 reps
- Core: Hanging leg raises 3 sets of 12

Wednesday - Upper Body Pull:
- Wide-grip pull-ups: 4 sets of 8 reps
- Chin-ups: 3 sets of 10 reps
- Inverted rows: 3 sets of 12 reps

Friday - Lower Body and Sprint Intervals:
- Bodyweight squats and walking lunges
- 5km outdoor trail run around campus lake.
